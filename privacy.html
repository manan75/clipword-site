Waitlist privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026. This page covers the waitlist on this website. The app will have its own privacy policy before it's released.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • The languages you tick, if any.
  • Which link brought you here (for example "linkedin"), if the link had a tag. This tells us which posts work; it says nothing about you.
  • The date you signed up.

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

What we use it for

Only to invite you to the Clipword closed test on Google Play and to tell you when the app launches. We may send a short question about which languages you study. We never sell or share your email, and never send it to advertisers.

Where it's kept

Sign-ups are stored in a private Google Sheet that only the Clipword developer can open. Google processes it as our service provider. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Removing your email

Reply to any email from us, or write to CONTACT_EMAIL, and your email is deleted. We delete the whole list once the test invites and the launch email have gone out.
